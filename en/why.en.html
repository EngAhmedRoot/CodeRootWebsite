01
👨‍💻

Starting path

Little Programmer

2 levels

Students learn these skills in parallel:

  • 🧠 Logical thinkingBreaking a problem into small, solvable steps.
  • ⚡ Electricity foundationsUnderstanding current, circuits, and safety through simple practice.
  • 🔌 ElectronicsExploring sensors, components, and how devices work inside.
  • 🧩 Scratch programmingCreating games and stories with commands, conditions, and loops.
  • 🤖 Artificial intelligenceUnderstanding AI ideas and using tools responsibly in practice.
  • 🎨 DesignUsing color and composition for interfaces and project characters.
A strong first foundationLogic, Scratch games, simple AI experiments, circuits and devices, plus design that powers each project.
02
💻

Expansion path

Advanced Programmer

3 levels

Students learn these skills in parallel:

  • 🧠 Logical thinkingBuilding clear algorithms and comparing possible solutions.
  • ⚡ Electricity foundationsConnecting electricity concepts to the everyday devices they use.
  • 🔌 ElectronicsMaking starter circuits and understanding components and sensors.
  • 🌐 HTML + CSS + jQueryBuilding organized websites with real design and interaction.
  • 🤖 Artificial intelligenceUsing AI tools thoughtfully in useful practical applications.
  • 🎨 DesignCreating interfaces that are beautiful and easy to use.
03
🚀

Mastery path

Professional Programmer

3 levels

Students learn these skills in parallel:

  • 🧠 Logical thinkingPlanning a solution for complex problems before writing code.
  • ⚡ Electricity foundationsConnecting electrical concepts with practical smart projects.
  • 🔌 ElectronicsUsing circuit components in interactive technology prototypes.
  • 🐍 PythonWriting programs with logic, data, and practical project goals.
  • 🤖 Artificial intelligenceBuilding deeper AI experiments and understanding their limits.
  • 🎨 DesignPresenting projects through intentional interfaces and visual identity.

The core idea

We do not teach one subject and move to the next. We build programming, algorithms, electronics, AI, and design together in parallel, so students can use all of these skills in practical projects.
